Delete your TapStats account

TapStats by David Hartley

In the app

  1. Open TapStats and sign in.
  2. Tap Account in the bottom bar.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

If you can't get into the app, email tapstats.support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Everything: your login and email address, and all your teams, matches and logged events. Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. Nothing is kept, apart from copies in our provider's routine backups, which are overwritten within 30 days.
