Support

We're a small team. We aim to reply within two working days.

Email support@tapstats.co.uk

To help us sort things quickly, please include the email address you signed up with, your phone model, and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps too.

Common questions

I didn't get my confirmation email

Check your spam or junk folder. It comes from TapStats (no-reply@mail.tapstats.co.uk). Still nothing after 10 minutes? Email us.

I've forgotten my password

Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen to get a 6-digit code by email. If your version of the app doesn't have that option yet, email us from the address you signed up with.

I lost signal during a match

Keep logging. Events are saved on your phone and sent to our servers when you're back in signal. Avoid logging out until they've synced: the app will warn you if anything is still waiting.

How do I delete my account?

See the account deletion page.
