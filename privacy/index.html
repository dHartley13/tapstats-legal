Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

The short version
  • We collect your email address and the team and match data you enter. That's it.
  • It's stored with our database provider, Supabase, in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • We don't sell or share it, show ads or use tracking or analytics.
  • You can delete your account and all your data at any time.

Who we are

TapStats is a rugby stats app run by David Hartley, an individual based in the United Kingdom. In this policy "we" and "us" means David Hartley. We are the "controller" of your personal data under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email tapstats.support@gmail.com.

What we collect

WhatDetailsWhy
Account detailsYour email address and password. Your password is stored in scrambled (hashed) form. We can't read it.To create your account, let you sign in and send account emails such as sign-up confirmation and password reset codes.

We don't collect your name, date of birth, location, contacts, photos or any player names. The app doesn't ask for them. Please don't type other people's personal details (such as players' full names) into free-text fields like team or opposition names.

What's stored on your phone

The app keeps your sign-in session and which team you last selected on your phone, in Android's encrypted secure storage. When offline saving is available, match events you log are held on your phone until they reach our servers. Logging out clears your session.

What we don't do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no selling or sharing your data for marketing, and no profiling.

Our lawful basis

We use your account, team and match details because we need them to provide the service you've signed up for (the "contract" basis). We keep technical logs because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and reliable.

Where your data is stored and who can see it

Your data is stored by Supabase, which provides our database and sign-in. It is hosted in Supabase's Frankfurt, Germany (EU) region. Account emails (sign-up confirmation and password reset codes) are sent by Resend from its Ireland (EU) region, so your email address passes through Resend when we email you. The UK recognises the EU as giving an adequate level of data protection, so no extra safeguards are needed for storing it there.

Supabase and Resend act only on our instructions under data processing agreements. Both are US companies and run on Amazon Web Services; if any of its staff or sub-processors access data from outside the UK or EU (for example for support), that is covered by the safeguards in their data processing agreements.

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google handles your download and any information on your Google account under Google's own privacy policy; we don't receive your Google account details.

We don't share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

How we keep it safe

  • All data travels between the app and our servers encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Database rules mean each coach's account can only read and change its own teams, matches and events.
  • Your sign-in session is stored in Android's encrypted secure storage on your phone.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, your teams, matches, events and login are deleted straight away and permanently. Copies in our provider's routine backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle, within 30 days. Technical logs are kept by Supabase for a short period and then deleted automatically.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Account → Delete account. If your version of the app doesn't show that option yet, email tapstats.support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we'll delete your account within 30 days (usually much sooner). More details are on the account deletion page.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • get a copy of your personal data;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • receive the data you gave us in a reusable format.

To use any of these rights, email tapstats.support@gmail.com. We'll reply within one month. It's free.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Age

You must be 16 or over to create a TapStats account. The app is designed for coaches. If you think someone under 16 has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
